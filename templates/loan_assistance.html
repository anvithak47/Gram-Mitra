🚀 Application roadmap

  1. Gather identity proofs and business project estimates.
  2. Submit primary application on the official portal / partner bank branch.
  3. Undergo field verification by nodal bank officers.
  4. Sanction & disbursement of loan funds to capital account.

⚠️ Gram Mitra uses a verified-rules approach. Scheme eligibility, participating lenders, accepted documents and any concession must be confirmed with the current official scheme/lender before application.
